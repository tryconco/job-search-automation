# 02 TRYCON MAS GUIDE

How to read the TradingView chart that uses the custom Trycon MAs indicator. This is the chart Josh sends. Read it exactly. Never invent a value that is not on the chart.

## The chart at a glance

* Platform. TradingView, mobile. Light background preferred, it reads faster and cleaner.
* Ticker. SPY or QQQ, top left with the full name. SPDR S&P 500 ETF Trust is SPY. Invesco QQQ Trust is QQQ.
* Under the ticker. The current price and the change on the day.
* Timeframe. Bottom left, usually 5m, the five minute.
* Time axis. Along the bottom, in Central time. Premarket is before 8:30 AM Central. The regular session is 8:30 AM to 3:00 PM Central. Judge the session in Central, never convert to Eastern.
* Current price. The dotted horizontal line with the green or red box on the right edge. The small number under that box is the countdown to the close of the live candle.
* Volume bars. Along the bottom, only when enabled.

## The five moving averages

The legend at the top left reads Trycon MAs 9 21 50 200 200. Those are the five lengths only. The live value of each line is the colored price tag on the right edge. Match every line by its color.

* EMA 9, light blue. The fastest line, hugs price the closest.
* EMA 21, blue. The second fast line.
* EMA 50, dark navy blue. The intraday trend anchor.
* EMA 200, amber or orange. A heavy line.
* SMA 200, purple. A heavy line that sits right next to the EMA 200.

Read each value off the right edge tag by its color. If a line is not tagged on the screenshot, say it is not clearly visible rather than guessing.

## How to use the lines

* The stack. Price above all five lines is a strong uptrend. Price below all five is a strong downtrend. Price tangled inside the lines is chop, which is a lower grade.
* Fast pair, EMA 9 and EMA 21. After a pullback, a reclaim and hold above the pair points long, a loss and fail below the pair points short. This chart has no VWAP, so the fast pair is the dynamic intraday reference in its place.
* Anchor, EMA 50. Price holding above it is intraday bullish, holding below it is intraday bearish.
* Heavy zone, EMA 200 and SMA 200. These two sit together and act as the major line in the sand. Reclaim and hold above them is a strong bullish signal. Rejection off them from below is a strong short signal. The distance from price to this zone tells you how extended the move is, and the zone is often the target or the wall.
* Crosses as confirmation. EMA 9 crossing up through EMA 21 and EMA 50 is momentum turning up, the reverse is momentum turning down. Treat a cross as confirmation next to a zone and a candle, never as a standalone trigger.

## Tie it to the method

Use these lines on top of the core read, the support and resistance zones, the candle patterns, and the break and retest. The moving averages confirm the trend and give dynamic levels. A clean setup is a closed candle confirmation at a zone, in line with the stack, with room to the next level. The heavy zone overhead or underfoot is usually the wall or the target.

## The candle countdown and developing candles

The green or red box on the current price line shows the last tick direction. The small number under it is the countdown to the close of the live candle. Use that countdown to know how much of the current candle is still unprinted.

A candle that is still counting down is developing. It is not a confirmed engulfing, pin bar, or inside bar until it closes. A developing candle can flip its shape before the close. Wait for the close, then read the pattern.

## What the chart cannot tell you

* A five minute chart cannot prove a daily, hourly, or fifteen minute trend. Those timeframes are not on this screenshot. Do not claim a higher timeframe trend from a five minute chart. If Josh wants the higher timeframe read, ask him to send that timeframe.
* Volume is evidence only when the volume bars are visible. If volume is not shown, do not claim a volume spike or thin volume.
* A price chart cannot show the option chain. It cannot verify a strike, premium, bid, ask, spread, delta, open interest, or contract volume. Read those off a chain screenshot only.

## Platform marks and extras

* The bell icons on the right price axis are Josh's existing alerts. Treat those prices as levels he already watches and reference them when relevant.
* Small circles or icons sitting on candles or on the MA lines are platform markers. Ignore them unless Josh asks.
* Premarket and after hours print on a lighter shaded background than the regular session.
* If the symbol box shows a full fund name, map it. SPDR S&P 500 ETF Trust is SPY. Invesco QQQ Trust is QQQ.

## Freshness rule

The screenshot is the freshest price available to this desk. Public web quotes run about fifteen minutes behind. Never let a web quote overrule what the chart shows. If the screenshot itself looks old, for example the countdown or the time axis shows a candle from much earlier than now, say the chart may be stale and ask for a fresh one instead of guessing.
